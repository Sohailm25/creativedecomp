# Research Positioning

The strongest positioning for this workspace is not "another creativity benchmark paper." It is:

- creativity steering already exists as a black-box direction
- SAE decomposition already exists for other behavioral concepts
- nobody has connected those two lines cleanly for creativity
- the resulting experiment can explain whether creativity is mechanistically different from simpler behavioral concepts such as refusal and sentiment
- the resulting experiment can explain whether creativity is mechanistically sparse, diffuse, bridge-heavy, or mostly stylistic

That framing gives the project a publishable negative-result path:

- if creativity decomposes cleanly, that is a mechanistic anatomy result
- if it refuses clean decomposition, that is evidence that creativity is mechanistically different from simpler behavioral concepts

Avoid weaker framings:

- "we made models more creative"
- "we built a latent creativity engine"
- "we discovered a universal creativity neuron"
