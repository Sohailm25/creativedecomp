# Safety Publication Policy

Safety is not the thesis of this repo, but safety-adjacent comparisons may appear.

## Rules

- If refusal, sycophancy, or honesty features are used as comparison baselines, label them as baselines rather than as a second thesis.
- Do not claim practical control over harmful behavior from feature identification alone.
- Do not publish prompts, steering settings, or instructions that materially strengthen misuse claims unless Sohail explicitly wants that lane explored.
- Keep any safety comparison narrow and in service of the creativity-mechanism question.
