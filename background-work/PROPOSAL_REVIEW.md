# Proposal Review

This note is the short-form review of the current experiment design.

## What Looks Strong

- The experiment is tightly scoped and mechanically specific.
- The default stack is realistic on the MacBook hardware you actually have.
- The novelty claim is credible because it bridges two active literatures that have not yet been connected cleanly.
- The project has a publishable negative-result path if creativity turns out to be diffuse or decomposition-resistant.

## What Could Still Go Wrong

- "Creativity" may collapse into style, verbosity, fantasy genre markers, or lexical diversity.
- Judge-based evaluation could flatter weak outputs.
- The chosen SAE suite may not represent creativity-relevant features cleanly.
- Feature alignment may fail to translate into causal output effects.

## What The First Real Implementation Must Prove

1. The dense creativity direction replicates on the local model.
2. The decomposition method respects signed contributions.
3. The top features do something causal under intervention.
4. Gains survive at least one frozen confirmatory split and one coherence-sensitive metric.
